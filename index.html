1. EXECUTIVE PRODUCT THESIS

Standard weather applications bombard users with raw numerical metrics (hectopascals, dew points, knot vectors) that demand excessive cognitive processing. WeatherMood reframes weather as an affective, emotional state—synthesizing multi-variable telemetry into intuitive atmospheric moods and mythic archetypes for instantaneous decision-making.
